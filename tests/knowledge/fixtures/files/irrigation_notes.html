Irrigation Controller Notes

Notes for the RainWise 8 controller in the garage (a fictional model).

ZoneAreaHeads
1Front lawnRotors

Watering Schedule

  • Lawns run Monday, Wednesday and Friday at 5:00 AM for 12 minutes.
  • Drip runs Tuesday and Saturday for 30 minutes.

Rain delay: press RAIN twice for a 48-hour pause.

Winterizing

Shut the backflow valve in November, then blow out the lines at 50 psi.
Restart in March.
